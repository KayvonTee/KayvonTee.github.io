Before I start this entry, there is something I've noticed about writing here that I wanted to point out.

Its tough to make my brain turn off. I am always wired, either thinking about something from the past, tasks that I have to complete, or self evaluating how I feel. These thoughts are constant. Taking time to put these thoughts into formable sentences has helped me process my thoughts and create a record of it. I can express and understand myself more here.

Now, to today's entry:
 
My senior year of college is about to begin. It's crazy to know that I only have 1 year left in my undergrad; the time has truly flown by.

Relating to the theme of college changes you, I have noticed a lot change in my life these past couple of years; these changes include new responsibilities, who I interact and associate with, clarity on post college plans, personal character development, and also some of my held beliefs. 

Today, I wanted to discuss a belief that I no longer hold true, and what changes I have made since. I used to believe that I could get along with anyone.

If you were to ask others to describe me, one thing they would likely mention is that I am easy to get along with, and I would say that this is true. I try to get along with everyone, knowing that with effort, I can make connections with people I meet and interact with. 

This effort making is distinct from being a people pleaser, however, as these individuals change themselves for others' satisfaction. I do not change myself for others; I just act like myself.

Of course, there have been times where I have had conflict with peers or friends. When there was conflict, I always wanted to resolve it. I used to belief I could find resolution with anyone. In doing so, I always questioned if I was doing something wrong when I had issues with my peers. Through interactions this past year with peers, I have learned that its okay to not get along with everyone.

Between 2 parties, it takes effort from both sides, and I have learned that there are some individuals that are uninterested in resolving problems they have with others. In college, I noticed that some of my peers would act nice to my face, but then would talk crap behind my back and take advantage of me. These people would like to complain even though they would get what they want. It's a lose-lose situation for me regardless of outcome. These are not individuals I want to spend my time and energy with; these types of people are incompatible with me. I've realized this belief that I could get along with anyone was only held in my optimism and naivety.

Last year, I was able to change who I surrounded myself with. My three roommates last year were amazing. I knew one of them from a past internship, and the other two were his friends from college. They are all a year older than me, and I started meeting and interacting with their friend circles. I consider myself very lucky and thankful for living with them last year. With a fresh reset and welcomed with open arms, I was able to remove myself from people who would not treat me well. 

With my roommates, our conversations would go beyond superficial, we would laugh and have fun, and we also supported each other in tough times. Everyone seemed to have more of a head on their shoulders, and I think this is one reason why we got along so well. After graduation last year, the four of us are not together anymore. I have 1 year left of undergrad, 2 of my roommates are working now and the other is going for grad school. My roommates from this past year are people that I intend to be friends and continue hanging out with, even if we are not together all the time anymore. And so far so good, I've been able to hang out with my roommates frequently this past summer.


My first day of senior year is tomorrow, and there are a few goals I have for the upcoming year. Firstly, I hope to gain more skills and experience to grow as an engineer through my senior capstone project and courses. Additionally, I hope to continue branching out and meet more people, especially now that I don't carry the burden of trying to get along with everyone. Lastly, I just want to enjoy my last year of college. After my summer as a co-op at an engineering company, I am already starting to feel that I am ready for the next chapter in my life post-college, but I don't want to get ahead of myself. This year is the last year that I will be surrounded by my friends. Everyone will go their separate ways after this year, just like with my roommates from last year. I want to make sure that I still cherish the time I get to spend with friends, even though it is coming to a close.
